01 — The studio

A sourcing partner,
not a brand.

The best product decisions happen before production starts.

BOLSUM works behind the scenes to turn concepts, tech packs and product ideas into commercially viable garments. We coordinate sourcing, development, sampling, costing and production follow-up with a practical, detail-focused approach.

The goal is simple: make the route from idea to production clearer, faster and more controlled.
